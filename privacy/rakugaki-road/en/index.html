3. Information the App stores on your device

The App can be used without creating an account, and we do not directly collect your name or similar information. The records below are themselves stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers (except for OS backups). However, some values, such as clearing a stage, the number of ★, the amount of ink used and opening a hint, are sent to Firebase Analytics as game progress events. The values sent as events are described in Section 4.

DataPurpose
Your rating for each stage (number of ★)Displaying your records and unlocking the next stages
Hints you have opened and explanations you have already seenLetting you review hints and explanations
Settings such as sound on/off and display languageKeeping your settings
A record of our requests for you to review the App on the storeAvoiding asking too often

Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device. However, copies stored by OS backup features (such as iCloud Backup or Google Backup) may persist in accordance with the settings and retention periods of the OS provider and may be restored when you reinstall.

You can delete the data on your device yourself at any time by uninstalling the App or by clearing the App's data in the OS settings (on Android, for example, "Clear data" under Settings > Apps > Doodle Road > Storage). We have no access to the data on your device and therefore cannot delete it for you.

The lines you draw in the App are used only while you play and to let you retry; they are never stored on your device or sent to us or to third parties. The App has no text-entry features, and nothing you type is ever sent as an event.

If you contact us, we collect your contact details such as your name and email address, the content of your enquiry and, where necessary, device and OS information, in order to respond. This information is deleted after a reasonable period once the enquiry has been resolved.

7. Managing consent and opting out

  • iOS (ATT): You choose whether to allow tracking in the ATT dialog, and you can change it at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown without using the advertising ID.
  • EEA, UK and other regions requiring consent: A consent form required by law is displayed. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via "Ad settings" in the in-game Menu (shown only in applicable regions). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Usage analytics: In regions where consent is required, Firebase Analytics does not collect usage data.
  • Additional OS settings: On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the OS settings (this is not the same as withdrawing consent).
