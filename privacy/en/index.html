Our apps can be used without creating an account, and we do not directly collect information such as your name. 4. Handling of In-App Data

Data you create or save within our apps, such as scan history, is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can delete this data within the app.

Uninstalling an app deletes the data on your device; however, copies stored by OS backup features (such as iCloud Backup or Google Backup) may persist in accordance with the settings and retention periods of the OS provider and may be restored upon reinstallation.

5. Provision to Third Parties / External Transmission

We do not provide collected information to third parties, except as required by law and except for transmission to the external service providers described in this Policy (such as Google).

Information transmitted through the SDKs described in Section 3 is handled by Google in accordance with its privacy policy. Depending on the nature of the processing, Google may process the information as an independent controller. Personalized advertising and cross-app tracking are carried out only in accordance with applicable law and your consent choices (iOS ATT and the consent form).

6. International Data Transfers

The destinations of the SDK transmissions described in Section 3 include Google servers located in the United States and other countries outside Japan. Google implements appropriate safeguards, including compliance with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Details of transfer destinations and safeguards are available in "Privacy and Security in Firebase" linked above.

7. Managing Consent / Opting Out

  • iOS (ATT): You can choose whether to allow tracking in the ATT dialog, and change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, etc.): A consent form required by law is displayed. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from "Settings > Ad privacy settings" in the app (shown only in applicable regions). Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Usage analytics: Firebase Analytics does not collect usage data in regions where consent is required.
  • Supplementary OS settings: On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in the OS settings (this is not itself a withdrawal of consent).
