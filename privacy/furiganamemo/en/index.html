5. Advertising in the App

The App displays a banner ad on the memo list, memo editor, word group and settings screens. In addition, you may choose to watch a video ad (a rewarded ad); doing so hides ads and unlocks the PDF export feature for 24 hours. Watching is entirely optional, and all other features of the App remain fully available if you do not.

In place of a Google AdMob ad, the banner slot may show a promotion for one of our other apps. Such a promotion only displays information we have prepared (app name, description, icon and a link to the store) and involves no collection or transmission of information about you.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. The advertising providers Google uses as partners are listed in Google's ad technology providers. Personalized advertising is carried out only in accordance with applicable law and your consent or permission choices (iOS ATT, and the consent form in the EEA and the UK). Where consent or permission has not been given, non-personalized (limited) ads that do not use the advertising ID are served instead.

6. External transmission

The only data the App transmits externally without action by you is the third-party SDK information described in Section 4. We operate no server of our own, and your memo content is never sent to us.

In addition, to show the promotions for our other apps described in Section 5, the App periodically retrieves the promotion information (app name, description and icon) from our website (qkserve.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages operated by GitHub, Inc.). As with viewing any web page, connection information such as your IP address reaches GitHub's servers during this retrieval; we do not collect or store it, and none of your activity or memo content is transmitted.

The "share", "export" and "PDF export" features only pass the converted text or file to the OS share sheet (the screen where you choose a destination app); whether and where it is sent from there is entirely your own choice and action. We never receive the content of anything you share or export.

7. In-app purchase

The App offers a one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchase, "premium", which permanently removes ads and unlocks the PDF export feature. Payment is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and we never receive your credit card number or other payment details. Your purchase status is stored on your device; after changing devices or reinstalling, you can restore it through your store account from "Settings > Premium > Restore purchases". Purchase history, refunds and related matters are handled in accordance with the privacy policies and terms of the respective store providers (Apple / Google).

8. How backups are handled

The App has no cloud-sync or backup feature of its own. On Android, automatic backup is disabled, so the App's data is not backed up to Google's servers. On iOS, if your device's iCloud Backup is enabled, a copy of the App's data may be stored on Apple's servers and restored when you reinstall. This copy remains even after you delete the App, so please remove it from your iOS backup settings if you no longer need it. If we add our own cloud-sync or backup feature in the future, we will revise this Policy and post a notice on this page.

9. Managing consent / opting out

  • iOS (ATT): You can choose whether to allow tracking in the ATT dialog shown on first launch, and change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If tracking is not allowed, neither we nor Google will use the IDFA to track you across apps.
  • Regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, etc.): A consent form is displayed through Google's consent management platform (UMP). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from "Settings > Privacy > Ad consent settings" in the App (shown only in applicable regions). Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): Collection is disabled by default and is enabled only for users in regions where consent is not required. For users in the EEA, the UK and other regions where consent is required, no usage data is collected regardless of the choices made in the consent form. All Google Consent Mode defaults are set to "denied".
  • Supplementary OS settings: On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads; on iOS the tracking setting above applies (neither is itself a withdrawal of consent).
