2. Scope and authoritative version

This Policy applies only to the App. Our other apps are covered by our common app privacy policy. This document is independent of the privacy policy on our corporate website. The Japanese version of this Policy is authoritative; if there is any discrepancy with a translation, the Japanese version prevails.

3. Information the App stores on your device

The App can be used without creating an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address or similar information. The data below is stored only in a database on your device and is never sent to our servers (except for the backups described in Section 5). We cannot access this data.

You can delete this data through actions in the App (deleting memos, folders or word groups, etc.), and uninstalling the App removes it from your device (except for the backups described in Section 5).

4. External transmission

The App has no internet-communication feature, and no data is ever sent to our servers. The "share" and "export" features only pass the converted text to the OS share sheet (the screen where you choose a destination app); whether and where it is sent from there is entirely your own choice and action. We never receive the content of anything you share or export.

5. How backups are handled

The App has no cloud-sync or backup feature of its own. However, if your device's OS backup (iCloud Backup / Google Backup, etc.) is enabled, a copy of the App's data may be stored on that OS provider's servers and restored when you reinstall. These copies remain even after you delete the App, so please remove them from your OS backup settings if you no longer need them. If we add our own cloud-sync or backup feature in the future, we will revise this Policy and post a notice on this page.

6. Third-party SDKs

The App does not incorporate any third-party SDK, including advertising SDKs, usage-analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics) or crash-reporting SDKs (such as Firebase Crashlytics). Neither we nor any third party collects usage data, advertising identifiers, or similar identifiers through the App.

The only exception is when you contact us: in that case we collect your contact details (such as name and email address), the content of your enquiry and, where necessary, device and OS information, for the purpose of responding. We delete this information after a reasonable period once your enquiry has been resolved.

7. Disclosure to third parties and external transmission

We do not provide collected information to third parties, except as required by law. As described in Section 4, the App performs no external transmission other than what you initiate yourself, so no information is provided to advertising or analytics providers.

8. International data transfers

Because the App never sends information to our servers or to any third party's servers, using the App does not involve any international transfer of data.

9. Additional information for users in the EEA and the UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

The App processes data only on your device, and we do not collect or store personal data through the App (except for the enquiry handling described in Section 6). Where we do collect information because you contacted us, the legal basis is taking steps in connection with responding to your enquiry, or our legitimate interest in providing an appropriate response; we delete it after a reasonable period once your enquiry has been resolved.

  • Access to data on your device: You can view, edit and delete your memos, word groups, folders and settings at any time directly in the App.
  • Your rights: Regarding any information you provide when contacting us, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection. Requests are accepted at the contact point in Section 12.
  • Complaints: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country.
  • Automated decision-making: We do not carry out automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal effects.

10. Users in other regions

Depending on the laws of your region, you may have rights such as access and deletion. Requests to exercise these rights are accepted at the contact point in Section 12.

11. Children
