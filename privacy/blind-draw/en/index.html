4. Online leaderboards and achievements

The App has online leaderboards for competing on clear time, and achievements for collecting tiles in the Tile Album. On iOS it uses Apple Game Center; on Android it uses Google Play Games services.

  • Only while you are signed in to the respective service, your clear time (best time) is sent to Apple's or Google's service. At launch, the best time stored on your device may be sent again so that clears made while offline still reach the leaderboard.
  • Likewise, only while you are signed in, the achievements you unlock by collecting tiles in the Tile Album (which achievements are unlocked) are sent to Apple's or Google's service. At launch, achievements already unlocked in the Tile Album on your device may be sent again. Per-tile times themselves are never sent. Sign-in takes place on each company's own screens; the App never handles your ID or password.
  • On the leaderboard screen, each company's service shows other users your player name and icon, your time and your rank. The App does not read or store your player name or icon.
  • The information sent is handled by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies. We do not separately store your player name or player ID.
    • Apple Privacy Policy
    • Google Privacy Policy
  • You can play the game without signing in (you just cannot take part in the leaderboards).

In place of a Google AdMob ad, the banner slot in the App may show a promotion for one of our other apps. Such a promotion displays information we have prepared (app name, description, icon and a link to the store). To measure effectiveness, when a promotion is shown or tapped it is recorded as a Firebase Analytics event described in Section 5 (including the ID of the promoted app); in regions where consent is required, only if you have consented.

To show these promotions, the App periodically retrieves the promotion information (app name, description and icon) from our website (qkserve.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages operated by GitHub, Inc.). 8. Managing consent and opting out

  • iOS (ATT): You choose whether to allow tracking in the ATT dialog, and you can change it at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and other regions requiring consent: A consent form required by law is displayed. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via "Ad Privacy Settings" on the Settings screen of the App (shown only in applicable regions). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Usage analytics: In regions where consent is required, Firebase Analytics does not collect usage data unless you consent.
  • Online leaderboards: If you sign out of Game Center / Google Play Games services, no further times or achievements are sent.
  • Additional OS settings: On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the OS settings (this is not the same as withdrawing consent).
