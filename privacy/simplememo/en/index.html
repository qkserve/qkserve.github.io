3. Information the App stores on your device

The App can be used without creating an account. We do not operate our own server for the App and never receive the contents of your notes. The data below is stored on your device and is never sent to us.

DataPurpose
Notes (text, checklists and canvases), including images you place on a canvas and link cards (title and image)Displaying, editing and searching notes
Character-count recordsShowing the calendar and graphs
SettingsKeeping your settings
Passcode (kept in the device's secure storage: iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)Locking the app and notes
Whether you have purchased Premium (verified and saved on the device)Hiding ads and lifting the PDF export limit
Records used to manage when the review request is shownAvoiding showing the request too often

On iOS, the data is stored in an App Group container, and the App's share extension and widgets read and write the same data. Uninstalling the App deletes this data from your device, but copies saved by the OS backup feature (iCloud Backup / Google Backup, etc.) remain according to that OS provider's settings and retention periods, and may be restored when you reinstall. However, passcode information kept in the iOS Keychain may remain on your device after the App is deleted.

Photos

Only the images you choose are placed on a canvas and saved on your device. The App does not read your entire photo library. Photos you take with the camera are likewise added to the canvas and stored on your device.

Lock

You can lock the app and individual notes with a passcode and biometric authentication. The passcode is kept in the device's secure storage. Biometric authentication is performed by the OS, and the App does not receive any biometric information such as fingerprints or faces. The lock hides content on the screen; it does not encrypt the contents of your notes. Please keep this in mind.

Exporting

You can use the OS share sheet to pass the text of a note or a PDF to other apps. You choose the destination, and how the data is handled after you pass it on is governed by the destination app's policies.

4. Cloud sync (optional)

Sync is optional and off by default. Only if you choose a sync destination does the App send your notes, checklists, canvases (including images), character-count records and records of deleted notes to your own cloud.

  • iOS: You can choose your own iCloud (CloudKit private database) or Google Drive.
  • Android: You can choose Google Drive.
  • Google Drive: The App uses only its own hidden app data area (appDataFolder; scope drive.appdata) and does not read any other files in your Drive. The token obtained through Google sign-in is stored on your device.
  • Synced data is placed only in your own iCloud or Google Drive, and we cannot see it.
  • With iCloud, the App receives notifications about changes made on your other devices as silent notifications with no sound or display. These are not promotional notifications.
  • Turning sync off does not automatically revoke the access you granted to Google. To revoke it, use your Google account settings.

Data in the cloud is handled by Apple and Google under their respective privacy policies.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Fetching link cards

When you paste a URL into a note, the App directly accesses that URL's page and the image in the page (og:image), fetches them, and shows the title and image as a card. The same applies to URLs received from other apps via sharing. This works like ordinary web browsing, so connection information such as your IP address is passed to the site being accessed. How that site handles it is governed by that site operator's policies. We do not receive the fetched information or the connection information.

7. In-app purchase

You can buy "Premium", a one-time purchase (no ads, no PDF export limit). Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play, and we do not receive payment information such as card numbers. Whether you have purchased is verified and saved on your device.

8. Review requests

The App may show the OS's standard review request (requestReview on iOS, In-App Review on Google Play). Whether it is shown and the submission of a rating follow the OS mechanism, and the App does not obtain the content of your rating.

9. What the App does not use

The App does not use analytics or crash collection (such as Firebase), location permissions, contacts, the microphone, or push notifications for promotion.

10. Disclosure to third parties and international transfers

We do not provide the information we obtain to third parties, except where required by law and for transmissions to the external service providers named in this Policy (Apple and Google). In the first place, we have no mechanism for receiving the contents of your notes.

The recipients in Sections 4 to 6 include Apple and Google servers located in the United States and other countries outside Japan. Google applies appropriate safeguards, including compliance with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Details of the destinations and safeguards are available in each company's privacy policy.

11. Managing consent and opting out

  • iOS (ATT): You can choose whether to allow tracking in the ATT dialog and change it at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The advertising ID (IDFA) is used only if you allow it.
  • EEA, UK and other regions requiring consent: A consent form from Google's UMP is shown. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the App's Settings screen (shown only in applicable regions). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Cloud sync: You can turn it off at any time in Settings. Data remaining in the cloud can be deleted from your iCloud settings or from "Manage apps" in Google Drive.
  • Supplementary OS settings: On Android, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in OS settings (this is not itself a withdrawal of consent).

12. Deleting your data

  • Deleting (uninstalling) the App removes the data on your device (except for the backups described in Section 3). However, passcode information kept in the iOS Keychain may remain on your device after the App is deleted.
  • Data in the cloud can be deleted by you from your iCloud settings or from "Manage apps" in Google Drive.
