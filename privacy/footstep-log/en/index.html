4. How location data is handled

The core function of the App is to record your routes and activities automatically, which requires location access in the background so that recording continues while the App is closed. Location is only collected if you grant permission in your operating system's dialog, and you can change or revoke it at any time in your device settings.

Location data is used solely for recording, classification and display on your device. It is never sent to our servers, and it is never used for advertising or ad targeting, nor provided to third parties for any other purpose.

5. How backups are handled

The App offers a feature that lets you back up your location, step and activity data to iCloud yourself (iOS). If you use it, the data is stored in your own iCloud account — storage provided by Apple — and we cannot access its contents.

In addition, if your device's OS backup (iCloud Backup / Google Backup) is enabled, a copy of the App's data may be kept on that OS provider's servers and restored when you reinstall. These copies remain even after you delete the App, so please remove them from your OS backup settings if you no longer need them.

6. How health data is handled

With your permission, the App reads step data from Apple Health (HealthKit) or Android Health Connect, and may write steps measured by the App back to them.

We never use health data for advertising or marketing, and we never provide or sell it to third parties. It is used solely to display step counts and statistics on your device. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's health settings.

10. Managing consent and opting out

  • Location, health and motion: You can change or revoke each permission at any time in your device settings. If you revoke them, the corresponding features (route recording, step collection, etc.) become unavailable.
  • iOS (ATT): You choose whether to allow tracking in the ATT dialog, and you can change it at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and other regions requiring consent: A consent form required by law is displayed. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings screen (shown only in applicable regions). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Additional OS settings: On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the OS settings (this is not the same as withdrawing consent).
