Three essentials

For the look of your photo.
For your privacy.

Find the plate, cover it naturally, and finish on your device. Everything you need before sharing a car photo, in one app.

01 / DETECT

AI estimates every corner

Automatically detect plates and estimate all four corners. The composite follows angled plates, while sectional checks make small plates in the background easier to find.

02 / COMPOSITE

Cover it with a plate, not a blur

Composite a solid, text, or image plate so the car photo keeps its look. You can also create your own plate with custom colors, borders, and text.

03 / PRIVATE

Your photo stays on your device

Detection, editing, and compositing run on your device. Your photo itself is not sent to a server, and the original is left unchanged.
