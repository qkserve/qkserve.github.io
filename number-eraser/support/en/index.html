Hide License Plate Support

Hide License Plate: AI Swap (shown on your device as NumberEraser) is an iOS / Android app that automatically detects license plates in car photos and hides them by compositing a plate of your choice. If you have any questions, please use the FAQ on this page and the contact point below.

FAQ

Q. Are my photos sent to a server?

No. Plate detection and compositing take place on your device. An edited photo leaves the App only when you save or share it yourself.

Q. A license plate was not detected

Use "Add" on the edit screen to add a frame, then drag its corners to fit the plate. Setting the detection level to "Lenient" reduces missed plates. Detection is not perfect, so please check that every plate is hidden before saving or sharing.

Q. The wrong area was detected

Setting the detection level to "Strict" reduces false detections. You can remove unwanted frames with "Delete".

Q. Is my original photo changed?

No. The edited photo is saved as a new image.

Q. Does location data stay in saved photos?

Saved images do not carry over the original photo's EXIF metadata, including location.

Q. Can I make my own replacement plate?

Yes. From the plate management screen you can create solid-color, text and image plates. Plates you create are stored on your device.

Q. I cannot save to my photos

Please check that saving to photos is allowed, and allow the App to access your photos in your device's Settings.

Q. How do I change my ad consent (EEA, UK, etc.)?

You can change it at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the App (shown only in applicable regions).
