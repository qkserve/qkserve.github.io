Doodle Road Support

Doodle Road is a drawing puzzle game for iOS and Android. Draw lines with your finger to guide the character "Ten" to the goal. Everything you draw has weight and falls, so the shapes you draw become bridges, slopes and lids. Please check the FAQ below or contact us.

FAQ

Q. How do I play?

Trace the screen with your finger to draw. When you are ready, press "▶ Start" and Ten starts walking. An open line becomes a thin bar, and a closed loop becomes a solid shape; both fall. Lay them across the ground as bridges or slopes, or roll a circle, to get Ten to the goal.

Q. How are ★ decided?

The less ink you use, the more ★ you get. The ink and the ★ are counted separately for the front and the back.

Q. What are the "front" and "back"?

There are 50 stages on the front of the notebook and 50 on the back. Play the front stages 1 to 50 in order; when you clear front stage 50, the page turns over and back stages 1 to 50 open. The back stages have two or three floors and branching paths. The front and the back are separate stages, and reaching the goal clears that stage.

Q. Are there hints?

From "See a hint" in the menu or on the miss screen, you can open a word hint and then example lines. A short video ad plays when you open them, and the hint opens when you watch it to the end. If you close the video before the end, the hint does not open. If the video cannot be shown, for example when you are offline or when the ad consent check has not been completed, the hint opens without an ad. Once opened, a hint can be viewed again at any time on that side.

Q. How do I unlock the EX stages?

Clear stage 50 on both the front and the back, and all three EX stages open.

Q. Can I transfer my progress to a new device?

Your progress is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, so it cannot be transferred to another device (it may be restored from an OS backup).

Q. Is what I play sent anywhere?

We never send the lines you draw. Your records are stored on your device, but some values, such as stage clears, the number of ★ and the amount of ink used, are sent to Firebase Analytics (Google) for usage analytics (not in regions where consent is required). For how data is handled for ads and usage analytics, please see the privacy policy.
