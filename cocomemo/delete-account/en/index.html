CocoMemo Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and related data for "CocoMemo" (ここメモ, iOS / Android), a location-based to-do reminder app provided by QuickServe Inc.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open "Settings > Recovery code" in the app and note your recovery code (used to verify your identity; do not share it with third parties).
  2. Contact us below with the subject "CocoMemo account deletion" and include the recovery code you noted.
  3. After verifying your identity, we delete the account and related data within 30 days of receiving the request and notify you when it is complete.
  4. To keep using the app after deletion, uninstall and reinstall it (a new anonymous account is created).

Copies contained in server backups are erased within 30 days of receiving the request.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • Memos and places: Can be deleted one by one in the app. When the creator deletes a shared memo, it also disappears for the people it was shared with.
  • Comments: Contact us above to request deletion of your comments.
  • Leaving a group: Available from the "Friends" tab in the app. After leaving, you can no longer view that group's shared memos.
  • Data on your device: Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your device (copies included in OS backups follow the OS provider's settings).
