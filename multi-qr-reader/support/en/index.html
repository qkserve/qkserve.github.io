Multi QR Reader Support

"Multi QR Reader" is an iOS / Android app that quickly scans QR codes and barcodes (EAN, Code128, Data Matrix, PDF417, and more) on a single screen and saves them to history. Please check the FAQ below or contact us with any questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I scan multiple codes at the same time?

Yes. When multiple QR codes or barcodes are visible to the camera, each detected code appears as a card on screen; tap a card to view its content.

Q. Can I scan a code from a photo (image)?

Yes. Tap the photo button on the scan screen and select an image from your device; the app analyzes any codes contained in the image.

Q. Where can I view or delete my scan history?

Open the history list from the history button on the scan screen. You can delete items one by one, or select multiple items / delete all in edit mode. History is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers (it may be included in OS backups depending on your device settings).

Q. How do I change my ad consent choices (EEA, UK, etc.)?

You can change them at any time from "Settings > Ad privacy settings" in the app (shown only in applicable regions).
