Notepad Support

"Notepad" is an iOS / Android note-taking app that is simple at first and becomes more useful the more you use it. Please check the FAQ below or contact us with any questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How do I set up sync?

Choose a sync destination in the app's Settings. Sync is optional and off by default. On iOS you can choose your own iCloud or Google Drive; on Android you can choose Google Drive. If you choose Google Drive, a Google sign-in screen opens. The app uses only its own hidden app data area and does not read any other files in your Drive. Synced content is placed only in your own iCloud or Google Drive, and we cannot see it.

Turning sync off does not automatically revoke the access you granted to Google. To revoke it, use your Google account settings.

Q. How do I restore my purchase?

After changing devices or reinstalling, you can restore the one-time "Premium" purchase (no ads, unlimited PDFs) with "Restore purchase" in Settings. Please use the same Apple account (iOS) or Google account (Android) you used to buy it. Payments are handled by the App Store / Google Play, so you can also check your purchase history in each store.

Q. I want to change my ad privacy settings

In the EEA, the UK and other regions that require consent, "Ad privacy settings" appears in the app's Settings, and you can change or withdraw your ad-related consent at any time (shown only in applicable regions). On iOS you can also change the tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you purchase Premium, ads are no longer shown.

Q. How are locked notes handled?

You can lock the app and individual notes with a passcode and biometric authentication. Locked notes show only their titles in the list and in search, and only the title in widgets. The passcode is kept in the device's secure storage (iOS: Keychain / Android: Keystore), and biometric authentication is performed by the OS. Note that the lock hides content on the screen; it does not encrypt the contents of your notes. If you forget the app passcode, you can create a new one by verifying with your device authentication (device passcode, Face ID, or fingerprint). We do not hold your passcode, so we cannot unlock it for you.

Q. How do I export my notes?

Use "Share" on the note screen to pass the text or a PDF to another app. You choose the destination in the OS share sheet. In the free version, you can create one PDF each time you watch an ad; Premium has no limit. To share a locked note, you must first verify your identity.

Q. Where is my data stored?

Notes, checklists, canvases, character-count records and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive the contents of your notes. Only if you turn sync on are they also stored in your own iCloud or Google Drive. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. Data in the cloud can be deleted from your iCloud settings or from "Manage apps" in Google Drive. See the Privacy Policy for details.
