Blind Draw Support

"Blind Draw" (Japanese name: Moupai) is an iOS / Android time-attack game in which you slide a finger over a hidden mahjong tile and work out which tile it is from your phone's vibration. Please check the how-to-play section and FAQ below, or contact us with any questions.

How to Play

  1. A tile with its pattern hidden lies on the upper part of the screen.
  2. Slide a finger over the tile. The phone vibrates only while your finger is on the pattern, and stays silent on bare parts. A circle around your finger flashes red in time with each vibration.
  3. When you know which tile it is, tap it among the 34 tiles lined up on the lower part of the screen.
  4. A correct answer moves you to the next question. A wrong answer costs a life and you answer again on the same tile. The game ends after 3 wrong answers.
  5. Clear 10 questions in the shortest time. Your best time is saved on your device, and also sent to the leaderboard if you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. My phone does not vibrate

Please check your device's vibration settings. On iPhone, turn on Settings > Sounds & Haptics > System Haptics. On Android, open Settings > Sound & vibration > Vibration & haptics (the name varies by device) and turn on touch feedback. Silent mode or battery-saving modes can weaken vibration on some devices. Devices that do not support fine haptics, such as many iPads, do not vibrate.

Q. Can I play without vibration?

Yes. The translucent circle around your finger flashes red over the pattern, so you can play by following the flashes even if nothing vibrates.

Q. The white dragon never vibrates

This is not a malfunction. The white dragon has no pattern, so it does not vibrate wherever you touch. A tile that feels like nothing is a clue in itself.

Q. The leaderboard does not show up

On iPhone, check that you are signed in under Settings > Game Center. On Android, check that you are signed in to Google Play Games. You can play without signing in, but your time will not appear on the leaderboard.

Q. What is "watch an ad to continue"?

When the game is over, you can watch an ad to continue with one life on the same tile. It can be used once per game, and the clock does not run while the ad is playing.

Q. Where is my data stored?

Your best time and settings are stored on your device. Sending a time to the leaderboard uses your platform's service (Game Center / Google Play Games). No account registration is needed. For advertising, usage analytics and crash reports, see the Privacy Policy.
